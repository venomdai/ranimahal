Our story

Meet Mr. Rabi Roshi

Rani Mahal Concrete Udhyog was founded by Mr. Rabi Roshi with a vision to provide quality construction materials and reliable service in Ratnanagar. After spending nearly 20 years working in Qatar, he returned to his hometown to create meaningful opportunities through local business and dependable concrete products.

With over five years of experience in the concrete industry, the business has supported many local projects and continues to serve the community with a commitment to quality, durability, and honest service.

The company’s mission is simple: strong products, dependable service, and long-term relationships built on trust.
